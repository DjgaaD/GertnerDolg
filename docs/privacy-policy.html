Privacy Policy for GertnerDolg

Last updated: October 18, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to GertnerDolg ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you use GertnerDolg, you may provide:

  • Personal contacts: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, and notes about people you track debts with
  • Financial information: Debt amounts, descriptions, and transaction history
  • Google Account: When you choose to use cloud backup, we access your Google account email for authentication

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We do not collect any analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics. The app does not track your behavior.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Local Storage

All your data (contacts, debts, transactions) is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. We do not have access to this data.

3.2 Google Drive Backup

If you choose to enable cloud backup:

  • Your data is encrypted and uploaded to your personal Google Drive in a hidden folder called appDataFolder
  • This folder is only accessible by our app and not visible in your Google Drive interface
  • We do not have access to your Google Drive or your backup data
  • Only you can access and restore your backups

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

  • Data is stored in your device's private app directory
  • Protected by Android's security model
  • No other apps can access this data

4.2 Cloud Storage

  • Backups are stored in Google Drive's appDataFolder
  • Encrypted in transit using HTTPS
  • Encrypted at rest by Google Drive
  • Only accessible with your Google account credentials

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT share, sell, or transfer your data to any third parties.

  • Your data stays on your device
  • Cloud backups go to YOUR Google Drive (not ours)
  • We have no servers that collect or store your data
  • We do not use analytics services
  • We do not use advertising networks

6. Google Drive API Usage

We use Google Drive API solely for:

  • Creating backup files in your personal appDataFolder
  • Restoring backup files from your appDataFolder
  • Listing your backup files
  • Deleting old backup files to manage storage

We do NOT:

  • Access any other files in your Google Drive
  • Read, modify, or delete your personal documents
  • Share your data with anyone
  • Use your data for any purpose other than backup/restore

7. Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required for Google Drive API communication
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To check if internet is available before attempting cloud operations
  • GET_ACCOUNTS: To integrate with Google Sign-In
  • READ_CONTACTS: Optional - to quickly add people from your contacts (only when you explicitly choose to)

8. Data Retention

  • Local data: Stored until you delete the app or manually delete records
  • Cloud backups: Stored in your Google Drive until you delete them. We automatically keep the 5 most recent backups.
  • Account data: If you sign out of Google, no data is retained about your account

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: All your data is visible in the app
  • Modify: Edit any information at any time
  • Delete: Delete individual records or all data
  • Export: Your data is stored in standard JSON format
  • Revoke access: Sign out of Google account at any time

10. Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.

12. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In: For authentication (governed by Google's Privacy Policy)
  • Google Drive API: For backup storage (governed by Google's Privacy Policy)

We recommend reviewing Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

13. Data Protection Compliance

We respect your privacy rights under applicable data protection laws, including GDPR (for EU users) and other regional privacy regulations.
