☁️ How to Enable Cloud Backup

  1. Open the app and go to Settings
  2. Tap "Sign in to Google for cloud backup"
  3. Choose your Google account
  4. Grant permissions for app data storage
  5. Done! Your data now automatically backs up to Google Drive

Your privacy is our priority:

❓ FAQ

Do I need a Google account?

No, the app works without a Google account. However, to use cloud backup and automatic data restoration features, you need to sign in with Google.

Is my data safe?

Yes! Your data is stored locally on your device and optionally backed up to YOUR Google Drive. We never have access to your data.

Will my data sync between devices?

You can restore your data on any device by signing in with the same Google account and tapping "Restore" in settings.

How much storage does it use?

Very little! The app itself is ~18 MB. Backups are usually less than 1 MB even with hundreds of records.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free with no ads!
